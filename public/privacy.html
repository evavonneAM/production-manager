Privacy Policy

Production Manager, operated by Alexander Matthews. Last updated October 16, 2026.

Production Manager is an internal tool used only by Alexander Matthews staff to track production work. It is not open to the public. Only people an administrator has added can sign in.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, role, department and preferred language.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We do not get your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or anything else in your Google account.
  • Work records: the tasks you clock in and out of, notes, photos and files you add in the app.
  • Client project information entered by staff, such as client names, addresses and site access details.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to let you sign in, show you the right screens for your role, track production and labor, and translate notes between English, Spanish and Russian.

Who can see it

Only signed-in Alexander Matthews staff, according to their role. We do not sell or share your information with advertisers or anyone else. It is stored with our service providers (Supabase for the database and sign-in, Vercel for hosting, DeepL for translating notes), who process it only to provide those services.

Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is checked by the database for every request. When someone leaves the company, their access is removed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.
